# Seoul Table Grill — Korean BBQ Night

A Korean BBQ cooking game set on a neon Seoul night street. You run one
table grill: take tickets from the order board, lay pork belly, beef
bulgogi or chicken on the charcoal grill, **flip each piece halfway**
(or one side burns), move it to the cutting board and snip it with the
scissors, then wrap it on the plate — lettuce leaf, rice, ssamjang —
and serve before the guest's patience bar empties. Kimchi is a banchan:
the bowl runs out mid-shift and has to be refilled at the banchan
station. Three strikes (or the end of the 180-second shift) ends the
night.

## How to play
- Click an ingredient bin (or press 1–8): meat goes to a free grill
  slot, wrap sides go straight onto the plate.
- Click a grilling piece to flip it; click a finished (green bar) piece
  to move it to the cutting board.
- Press **C** to scissor-cut the piece on the board when the ticket asks
  for cut meat, then click the board to plate it.
- Click **BANCHAN REFILL** when the kimchi bowl is empty.
- **Space / Enter** or the SERVE button serves the plate; **Backspace**
  clears it. **R** restarts after the shift ends.
